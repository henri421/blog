| $p$ | $E_k$ | (8.4) $k\,(\gamma F)^p$ | (8.5) $\gamma\,k\,F^p$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2/3 | 50 × 2^(2/3) = 79,37 | 50 × 3^(2/3) = 104,00 | 1,5 × 79,37 = 119,06 |
| 1 | 100 | 150 | 150 |
| 2 | 200 | 50 × 9 = 450 | 300 |
